# Master Index & Exam Strategy

**Unit:** Sequences and Series, Proof, Counting Methods, and the Binomial Theorem
**Course:** IB Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches HL
**Source:** Official Department of Mathematics Unit Plan

This page is the map for the entire study series. Read it first, then work through each topic page in order using the **Next Topic** link at the bottom of every page.

**Key finding:** this unit includes a full 10-lesson block on **Mathematical Proof** — deductive/LHS-RHS proof, induction, disproof by counterexample, and proof by contradiction. This strand sits between sequences and counting, so it's the easiest to forget when revision time is short. Do not skip it.

## Why This Matters

Your grade weighting places heavy emphasis on this single unit test relative to quiz averages. Every sub-topic above is examinable on both papers — the earliest-taught material (sequences, proof) is where basic marks are most often lost through rustiness, not difficulty.

## How to Use This Site

1. Work through pages in numeric order once, fast — this rebuilds the whole unit from the beginning, not just the most recent lessons.
2. Every page has: theory, boxed formulas, fully worked examples, the exact question types examiners use, common mistakes specific to that sub-topic, and a self-test with answers.
3. Cross-reference with your own old tests and practice packages — familiar-feeling self-test questions are a strong signal of what gets reused.
4. The final page, **Master Formula Sheet & Old-Test Strategy**, ties everything together.

## Paper Structure Reminder

- **Paper 1 (no GDC):** algebraic manipulation of sequence formulas, factorial/counting problems with clean numbers, binomial general-term questions, short proof questions.
- **Paper 2 (GDC allowed):** longer context problems — financial applications, sequences requiring numerical solving or graphing, and fuller induction proofs.
