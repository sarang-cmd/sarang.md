# Trigonometric functions and graphs

> **SL 3.7 · AA SL foundation (also studied at HL)** · Original Sarang.md course lesson, not an IB publication.

## Understand the idea

The sine and cosine graphs repeat after a fixed horizontal period. Multiplication outside sets amplitude; multiplication of the input changes the period. A vertical translation changes the midline, so the range is centered on that line.

## Formula, meaning and conditions

For $y=a\sin(bx)+d$ with $b\neq0$, amplitude is $|a|$, period is $2\pi/|b|$, midline is $y=d$, and range is $[d-|a|,d+|a|]$. Horizontal shifts enter as $b(x-h)$; keep parentheses.

## Worked example

For $f(x)=2\sin(3x)+1$, the amplitude is $2$ and period $2\pi/3$. The midline is $y=1$, giving range $[-1,3]$. At $x=0$, $f(0)=1$; at $x=\pi/6$, $3x=\pi/2$ and $f=3$, a maximum. These anchor points determine a sketch of one cycle.

## Why the method works

For $a\sin(b(x-c))+d$, the magnitude $|a|$ is amplitude, $2\pi/|b|$ is period when $b\neq0$, and $d$ is the midline. The sign of $a$ changes orientation. A phase shift alters where a cycle starts, not its height or duration. Domains can restrict how many peaks are relevant.

## A contrasting worked route

The graph $y=2\cos(3x)+1$ oscillates between $-1$ and $3$, about midline $1$, with period $2\pi/3$. At $x=0$ it begins at a maximum of $3$. A proposed amplitude of $3$ confuses the upper value with its distance from the midline.

## Transfer and validation

If a model predicts negative depth or temperature below a physical limit, separate the mathematical graph from the realistic interpretation. Give a unit to the horizontal period. When fitted from data, several trigonometric formulas may produce the same graph through equivalent phase shifts.

## Exam lens

Paper 1 style: label one full period and the shifted midline rather than drawing an unscaled wave. This is a practice lens, not a prediction of an examination question.

## A mistake worth catching

The period of $\sin(3x)$ is $2\pi/3$, not $6\pi$. Input scaling is inverse.

## Try it yourself

Give the amplitude, period and range of $y=3\cos(2x)-1$.

## Checked answer

Amplitude $3$, period $\pi$, range $[-4,2]$.
